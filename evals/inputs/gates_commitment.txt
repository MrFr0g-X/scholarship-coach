Since my childhood I have always been passionate about helping people and making a difference in my community, because I believe that helping others is the most important thing a person can do in their life and it gives life meaning. In 2020, during the pandemic, I noticed that many elderly people in my neighbourhood in Irbid could not go out to buy their medicines and food, and many of them lived alone without any family to help them. I decided to start a volunteer initiative with my friends from university to deliver medicines and groceries to them. At first we were only five volunteers, but after I posted about it on Facebook, more people joined us and we reached around 60 volunteers within two months. We created a WhatsApp hotline and a schedule so that every elderly person was visited twice a week. We also partnered with two local pharmacies who gave us discounts. Over eight months, we delivered more than 3,000 orders to around 180 elderly people. This experience taught me the value of teamwork and the importance of communication, and it showed me that small actions can have a big impact. Furthermore, I also volunteered in a refugee camp teaching English to children every Saturday for one year, which was a very meaningful experience for me. Moreover, I donate blood regularly. I believe that all of this makes me an excellent candidate for the Gates Cambridge Scholarship because I am committed to improving the lives of others.
