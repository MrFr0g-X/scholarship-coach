# Mode: Elevator pitch

The "elevator pitch": you meet an important person in a lift, the ride takes about 30 seconds, and you get one chance to introduce yourself. This is also the interview opener ("Tell us about yourself").

## Structure
| Seconds | ~Words | Beat |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | ~12 | Who you are: role, field, place |
| 5-15 | ~25 | **One proof**: the strongest number or recognition |
| 15-25 | ~25 | **The purpose beyond yourself**: the problem you work on and for whom |
| 25-30 | ~13 | Why this program or opportunity, now |

That's about **75 words for 30 seconds** and about **150 words for 60 seconds**. For the 60-second version, add a second proof and a sentence on the 5-year goal.

## Rules
- Build it only from `applicant/facts.yaml` and the story bank.
- Write it to be **spoken**: short sentences, no parentheses, numbers that are easy to say ("over 3,000", not "3,174").
- Read-aloud test: time it at a normal pace (about 150 words per minute).
- Deliver both versions plus a one-line "hook" version for networking events.
- Offer an Interview-mode drill: "Tell us about yourself", with scoring.
