# Mode: Reframe ("how you tell it beats what you did")

Two people can do the same volunteer work and write very different essays. One lists the task. The other shows its full ripple: who benefited, what problem it touched, what it taught them. Use this mode whenever a user says "I haven't done anything special" or when a story reads flat.

## Input
One experience in the user's words, in any language: a job, volunteering, a family responsibility, a university project, even a part-time job.

## Process
1. **Restate the bare facts** as a neutral list. Ask for any missing basics: when, how long, how many people, who benefited.
2. **Map the ripple: who benefited, and how far down the chain?**
   - Direct: the people you served.
   - Second order: their families, colleagues and community.
   - System level: the environment, the economy, health, education, gender, institutions.
3. **Find the angles.** Offer **3-5 angles as questions**, never as claims. Examples:
   - "Did this protect anyone's health or the environment?"
   - "Did it create income or opportunity for someone, especially women or youth?"
   - "Did you fix a process others had accepted?" (initiative)
   - "Did you persuade or train anyone?" (leadership)
   - "What did it teach you about a bigger problem in your country?" (the wider understanding that makes a modest achievement meaningful)
   - "What would have happened if nobody had done it?"
4. **Match each angle to its best home:** the essay type (leadership, networking, career, challenge, added value) and the matching SDG, if the link is honest.
5. **Mark the risk.** Every angle must be **true and defensible**. If an angle needs a fact the user hasn't confirmed, tag it `NEEDS CONFIRMATION`. Reframing changes the lens, never the facts.

## Output
```markdown
## Reframe: <experience>
**Bare facts:** …
**Ripple map:** direct → second-order → system
**Angles (confirm before using):**
1. <angle as a question> → best for <essay type> · SDG <n> · needs: <fact>
…
**Strongest angle for <target essay>:** <why, in one line>
**Next:** answer the confirmations, then I'll add it to your story bank as a STAR story.
```

Once confirmed, write the story into `applicant/story-bank.md` and add any new facts to `applicant/facts.yaml`.
