# Stipendium Hungaricum

> **Verify live:** the timeline below is the 2025/26 cycle as published (deadline 15 January 2026, 2 PM CET). The next cycle will have new dates. The 600-word budget is a working default; follow any limit the portal or university gives.

## Official sources
- Apply: https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/apply/

## Who it's for
- Bachelor's, master's, PhD and one-tier programs in Hungary, for citizens of partner countries.
- **Nomination by your country's Sending Partner is mandatory.** "Only the nominated applicants can proceed."

## Process (as published for 2025/26)
1. November-December: choose programs. "Only 2 choices are allowed", out of 900+ programs at about 30 institutions.
2. December: contact your Sending Partner, register and upload documents.
3. **15 January, 2 PM CET:** application deadline.
4. February: Sending Partners submit nomination lists.
5. **March-May: university entrance exams or interviews.** You need at least **56/100** to be considered.
6. June: results.

## Essays and what each one tests
### motivation_letter
- Why the chosen program and university in Hungary, your preparation, and your plans back home. The Sending Partner may have its own priority fields.
- With only 2 choices, the letter should match each choice (Adapt mode).

**Beat plan (≈ 600 words):**

| Budget | Beat |
|---|---|
| ~80 | Hook |
| ~150 | Preparation |
| ~200 | Why this program and university (named specifics) |
| ~170 | Return plan and impact for your country (it's a bilateral cooperation program) |

## Interview / selection
- The university's entrance exam or interview is the decisive stage.
- Prepare with Interview mode, using a university-admissions persona plus subject questions.
