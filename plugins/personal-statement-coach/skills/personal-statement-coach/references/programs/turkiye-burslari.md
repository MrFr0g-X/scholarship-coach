# Türkiye Bursları

> **Verify live:** the letter-of-intent format (questions and limits) appears in the online portal and can change. **3,000 characters** is the widely reported limit, not confirmed on an official page. Graduate research sections are reported as 75-300 words each. Confirm in the portal.

## Official sources
- Application process: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/en/page/prospective-students/application-process
- Evaluation and selection: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/evaluationandselectionprocess
- Portal: https://tbbs.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr

## Who it's for
- Undergraduate, master's, PhD and research programs in Türkiye.
- Online application, free of charge.

## Selection (official)
1. **Preliminary assessment:** minimum academic achievement, age limits, documents.
2. **Expert evaluation:** "academic status, previous qualifications, academic interests, career goals, **consistency of preferences**, **content of the letter of intent**, and participation in social activities."
3. **Tests:** undergraduate only; some fields need English.
4. **Interview:** face to face, **15-30 minutes**. It covers document verification, why you applied, academic knowledge and career objectives.
5. **Final assessment.**

## Essays and what each one tests
### letter_of_intent (portal questions, about 3,000 characters)
- **Tests:** motivation, **consistency between your field, your program preferences and your career goals**, and social engagement.
- **Tip:** "consistency of preferences" is scored, so the programs you choose must tell one coherent story that the letter explains.
- Graduate applicants may also get research-proposal sections (title, the issue, method, structure, academic contribution, literature).
