# Global Korea Scholarship (GKS), Graduate

> **Verify live:** download the year's official **Application Guidelines** from Study in Korea (studyinkorea.go.kr). Forms, limits and tracks (Embassy vs University) are fixed there. The ~1,000-word figures come from widely reported guidance on the official forms. Confirm in the current PDF.

## Official sources
- Study in Korea (GKS guidelines and forms, published each year): https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr

## Who it's for
- Master's and PhD study in Korea.
- **Embassy track** (via the Korean embassy in your country) or **University track** (direct to a university).
- Rules on how many tracks or universities you can apply to are in the guidelines.

## Essays and what each one tests
These are two separate documents that must be **distinct but complementary**.
### personal_statement
- Your background, your interest in the field, your personal motivation, and character and growth.
- V-SPICE fits naturally here.

### study_plan
- **What** you will study, your academic objectives, research interests, and the plan for language study (Korean training year) and the degree.
- **Why Korea** is the right place.
- Your plans after graduation.
- Name the universities and programs, and how they fit.

**Trap:** repeating the same content in both. Use the personal statement for *who you are and why*, and the study plan for *what, how and where next*. Run `check_consistency.py` on the pair.

## Other documents
- Application form, research proposal (where required), recommendation letters, letter of invitation (some tracks), and the applicant agreement.
