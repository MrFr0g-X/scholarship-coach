# MEXT Scholarship, Research Students (Embassy Recommendation)

> **Verify live:** forms change each fiscal year. The **Field of Study and Research Plan** is reported to be limited to **4 pages** on the official form; the 2,000-word budget approximates that. Download the current forms from Study in Japan and submit through the Japanese embassy or consulate.

## Official sources
- Study in Japan, MEXT embassy recommendation (research students): https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/smap-stopj-applications-research.html

## Who it's for
- Graduate-level research students (often leading to a master's or PhD) in Japan, nominated by the Japanese embassy in your country.
- The embassy runs the first screening: documents, written tests and an interview.

## Essays and what each one tests
### research_plan (Field of Study and Research Plan, official form, about 4 pages)
- A detailed proposal: the research topic, background and significance, your past work, methodology, the expected outcome, and **why Japan** (named universities and professors, or labs), plus your future plans.
- It's a research proposal more than a personal statement. Course techniques still apply: a hook from your experience, numbers, and a beyond-self impact.
- The **Placement Preference Form** names the universities and advisors. Keep it consistent with the plan (`check_consistency.py`).

## Other documents
- Application form, placement preference form, certificate of health, recommendation letter, transcripts and graduation certificates.
