# University personal statement / Statement of Purpose (generic)

> **Verify live:** every university and program sets its own limit. UK master's statements are often 500-1,000 words or ~4,000 characters; US SOPs are often 1-2 pages. Some UK courses don't require a statement at all (e.g. Sheffield's MPH lists none as required). Read the course page.

## Who it's for
Direct university applications, separate from any scholarship.

## Essays and what each one tests
### personal_statement (UK-style) / sop (US-style)
- **Tests:** academic preparation, motivation for *this* program, research or professional fit, and career goals.
- US SOPs lean toward research interests and faculty fit.
- UK statements lean toward course fit and career.

**Beat plan (≈ 650-1,000 words):**

| Paragraph | Job |
|---|---|
| ¶1 | Hook: the moment or problem that pulled you into the field |
| ¶2 | Academic preparation: courses, research, methods, grade trend (only if strong) |
| ¶3 | Practical experience as STAR, with numbers |
| ¶4 | **Why this program:** named modules, faculty, labs and resources, each tied to a need from ¶1-3 |
| ¶5 | Goals: short and long term, SMART, beyond yourself |

**Research rule (Ground rule 5):** module names, professors and course facts must be checked on the official course page during the session and cited, never recalled from memory.

## AI policy
Varies by university. Many ask for a declaration of originality. Check the application portal.

## Verify-live checklist
- [ ] Is a statement required, and what's the limit (words or characters)?
- [ ] Current module list and course title (MSc vs MA vs MPH, etc.).
- [ ] Any specific questions the portal asks instead of a free statement.
