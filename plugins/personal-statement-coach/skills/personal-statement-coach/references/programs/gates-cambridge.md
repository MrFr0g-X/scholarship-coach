# Gates Cambridge Scholarship

> **Verify live:** questions and limits are from the official how-to-apply page on `last_checked`. **Limits are in characters**, so run `check_draft.py --program gates-cambridge --essay <key>`.

## Official sources
- How to apply: https://www.gatescambridge.org/apply/how-to-apply/

## Who it's for
- Full-cost postgraduate study at Cambridge for applicants from outside the UK.
- Select Gates funding in the Cambridge postgraduate application.

## Essays and what each one tests
These are the official questions (checked 2026-10-03):

| Key | Question | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| academic_excellence | "Please describe how, through your education and past experiences, you have displayed academic excellence." | ~200 words / 1,400 chars |
| course_justification | "Please explain why you want to study this specific course at the University of Cambridge, and how it will facilitate your future plans." | ~200 words / 1,400 chars |
| commitment_to_others | "Please provide an example, or examples, of how you have demonstrated a commitment to improving the lives of others. **Please explain why this is important to you and how it is relevant to your future plans.**" | ~300 words / 2,100 chars |
| leadership | "Please provide an example, or examples, of how you have demonstrated a capacity for leadership. **This could be by initiating a change or bringing others together to achieve a goal. How will this help you make an impact in the future?**" | ~300 words / 2,100 chars |

- PhD applicants also submit a **research proposal**.
- A **third reference** assesses suitability against the four criteria.

**Planning tips:**
- Allocate different stories to the four answers (holistic review), and run `check_consistency.py`.
- *commitment_to_others* is the beyond-self test in pure form. Use STAR, naming who benefited, with numbers. **The question has three parts: the example(s), why it matters to you, and the link to your future plans.** An answer without the "why" and "future plans" parts misses a third of the question.
- *leadership* also asks how it will help you **make an impact in the future**. End on that.
- *course_justification* needs Cambridge specifics (supervisor, lab, modules) tied to your future plans.

## AI policy (University of Cambridge postgraduate admissions, checked 2026-10-03)
Source: https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/apply/how/ai-postgraduate-applications
- "AI must not be used when writing personal statements and CVs." This extends to "the use of AI to help your use of English."
- "AI must not be used to impersonate or assist you during an interview."
- For research proposals: "Appropriate use of AI tools to support the preparation of research proposals is permitted," and "content generated by AI should be acknowledged."
- AI-generated false claims about personal achievements "will be treated like any other application fraud."

The Gates questions are answered inside the Cambridge application and are personal-statement-like, so **treat them as covered by the personal-statement prohibition** unless the portal says otherwise.

**What the skill does:** say this once. Recommend Coach, Plan, Review (as feedback) and mock interview practice, with the user doing all the writing and the English. Don't offer Write or Polish (language editing) for these answers.
