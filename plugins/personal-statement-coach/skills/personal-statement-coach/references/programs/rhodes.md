## Who it's for
- Postgraduate study at Oxford.
- **Eligibility depends entirely on constituency.**
- ⚠️ **The Global Rhodes Scholarship is suspended.** The Rhodes Trust Board "will not be awarding Global Scholarships going forward" (reported April 2026; source: https://cherwell.org/2026/04/26/rhodes-scholarship-suspends-global-constituency-applications/). Applicants from countries without their own constituency (e.g. **North Africa**, most of Europe, South and Central America) are currently **ineligible**.
- **Check first, before any writing:** does the applicant's country belong to a current constituency? Use the eligibility checker on rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk. If not, say so plainly and suggest alternatives (Chevening, Commonwealth, Gates Cambridge, Clarendon, etc.).

## Essays and what each one tests
### personal_statement
- A "truthful and authentic" narrative. "It will serve you best if you speak in your own voice," so selectors get "a fuller picture of who you are".
- Map it to the current selection criteria (read the criteria page).

### academic_statement
- Your academic trajectory and why this Oxford course.

## AI policy (Rhodes Trust, May 2026)
- **Unacceptable:** "generating substantive content or replacing your own analysis and voice with AI generated outputs."
- **Acceptable examples:**
  - suggestions for reducing a narrative (e.g. from 2,000 to 1,000 words),
  - checking spelling or grammar,
  - writing a summary of your narrative to help you synthesise your thoughts,
  - help with structuring your statement.
- **Disclosure is required.** Applicants say who and what helped (advisors, friends and family, alumni, GenAI tools), and for GenAI "which tool was used, and how you asked it to help/what prompts were used". Disclosing "will not disadvantage you".
- **Attestation:** each statement is "my own work and … a wholly accurate and fair representation", where "own work" means "your original experiences, thinking, and judgment, even where AI tools might have supported expression".
- Mentors and friends "must not write or edit" the statements.
- AI may be used to **practise** for the interview, but never **during** any Rhodes interview or social engagement.

**What the skill does for Rhodes:**
- Coach, Plan, Review (as feedback), Tighten (as suggestions), Polish (spelling and grammar) and Interview prep all fit within the policy.
- **Write mode would generate substantive content, which Rhodes rules out.** Say so plainly once and recommend Coach → Plan → the user writes → Review.
- Remind the user to keep a log of the tool and prompts for the disclosure.

## Interview / selection
- A panel interview of about 30-45 minutes.
- Some constituencies add social or other stages.
