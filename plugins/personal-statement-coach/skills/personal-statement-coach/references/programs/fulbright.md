# Fulbright Foreign Student Program

> **Verify live:** the program is run **country by country** by binational Fulbright Commissions or US Embassies (AMIDEAST in parts of MENA). Eligibility, limits and deadlines vary widely. The 1,000-word budgets are placeholders, since many countries say "about one page". Use the applicant's country page.

## Official sources
- Apply (choose your country): https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/apply
- Example, Egypt: Binational Fulbright Commission, https://fulbright-egypt.org/ (see its Egyptian Student Program pages and grant application instructions)

## Who it's for
- Master's or PhD study in the US.
- Applications go through the home-country commission or embassy, which also handles the interview.

## Essays and what each one tests
### personal_statement
- A narrative that gives the reader insight into **who you are**: background, the challenges you've overcome, and what shaped your worldview and values.
- It is *not* a second study plan. Use V-SPICE: vulnerability, perseverance, initiative.
- Fulbright is a **cultural exchange** program, so show openness, how you'll represent your country, and what you'll bring back.

### study_objectives (Study/Research Objectives)
From the commonly published guidelines:
- a clear, detailed description of your study or research objectives and **why** you want to pursue them;
- your degree objective, major field and specialised interests;
- the kind of program you expect to undertake;
- how it fits your **previous training** and your **future objectives**.

**Beat plan (one page ≈ 600-800 words):**

| Budget | Beat |
|---|---|
| ~120 | The problem or question that drives you |
| ~200 | Preparation so far |
| ~250 | The program you'll pursue (specialisation, the kind of courses and research) |
| ~180 | Return and impact at home, plus the exchange dimension |

## AI policy
Varies by country. Check your commission's instructions and declarations.

## Interview / selection
- A commission or embassy panel interview, often in English, about your objectives, your fit and your role as a cultural ambassador.
- Practise with the mock interview mode using the Fulbright persona.
