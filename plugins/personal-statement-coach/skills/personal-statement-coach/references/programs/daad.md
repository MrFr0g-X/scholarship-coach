# DAAD Scholarships (Germany)

> **Verify live:** each DAAD program has its own call for applications. Read the call in the scholarship database for the exact documents, deadline and format. The `motivation_letter=900` limit is a working budget for "1-3 pages". Confirm the page limit in the call.

## Official sources
- Overview: https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/daad-scholarships/
- Information for applicants (documents, criteria, AI rule): https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/information-for-scholarship-applicants/
- Scholarship database (calls per program): https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/

## Who it's for
- Master's, PhD and research stays in Germany, with many country- and field-specific programs.
- EPOS: development-related postgraduate courses for professionals from developing countries. EPOS usually requires work experience; check the call.

## Essays and what each one tests
### motivation_letter: letter of motivation ("at least one and no more than three pages")
**What it must address (DAAD):**
- education, skills and knowledge
- **academic motivation for the chosen program**
- **reasons for applying for DAAD funding**
- personal interests and extracurricular achievements

DAAD wants it "in your own words" to show your personality.

**Selection criteria (DAAD):**
- **Professional qualification:** grades, how they developed, languages.
- **Project quality:** originality, relevance, feasibility, preparation.
- **Applicant potential:** motivation, career prospects, extracurricular engagement.
- Special circumstances (disability, chronic illness, caregiving, refugee status) are considered "to compensate for disadvantages". Mention them honestly if relevant; this is the "challenges in proportion" rule.

**Beat plan (about 2 pages ≈ 800-900 words):**

| Budget | Beat |
|---|---|
| ~120 | Hook: the problem in your field or country that drives you |
| ~200 | Academic preparation: degree, key courses, grade trend, languages |
| ~200 | Experience (STAR) and extracurriculars, with what you developed from each |
| ~200 | Why this program in Germany: named modules, chairs and partners, and **why DAAD funding** |
| ~130 | Return plan and impact (SMART, beyond yourself) |

**EPOS add-on:** state the development relevance of your work, your employer's support or return plan, and the concrete problem at home that the course helps solve.

## AI policy
DAAD **permits AI tools if their use is clearly indicated**: write "Produced with the aid of (tool used)" in the documents. Applicants declare that the application was "prepared independently, with proper attribution of resources and adherence to the principles of good scientific practice."

**What the skill does:** whenever it drafts or edits text for DAAD, it reminds the user to add the disclosure line naming the tool.

## Interview / selection
- Independent volunteer selection committees.
- The best applicants "may also be required to introduce themselves personally", on site or virtually, depending on the program.

## Common mistakes specific to DAAD
- No reason given for needing **DAAD funding** specifically.
- A generic "Germany has excellent education" line instead of named modules and chairs.
- Ignoring the specific call's format (CV template, signed and dated letter, page limits).
- Forgetting the AI disclosure line when AI helped.
