## Who it's for
- A one-year UK master's for future leaders.
- **At least 2 years' work experience** (2,800 hours) after the undergraduate degree.
- The undergraduate degree must qualify the applicant for a UK master's.
- Applicants **commit to returning home for at least 2 years** afterwards.
- They **apply to 3 different eligible UK courses** and need at least one unconditional offer by the conditional-offer deadline.
- Citizenship rules apply, and dual British citizens are excluded.

## Essays and what each one tests
These are the official assessment wordings (application-criteria page, checked 2026-10-03).

### leadership: leadership and influencing (300)
- **Tests:** "examples of taking the lead and influencing others" with **"clear results"**. Influence, not titles.
- **Beat plan:**

  | Budget | Beat |
  |---|---|
  | ~60 | Hook: a high-signal moment, or a rapid-fire list of recognitions |
  | ~15 | Pivot: "it took an N-year journey" |
  | ~150 | One STAR story: the problem noticed → what you set up → the people mobilized → measurable results |
  | ~45 | Growth to the present scale |
  | ~30 | Forward link + "Most importantly…" |
- **Must include:** numbers for scale, people who followed or adopted your idea, a result.

### networking: networking skills (300)
- **Tests:** "how you've built strong working relationships and how collaboration led to **real, measurable outcomes**."
- **Beat plan:**

  | Budget | Beat |
  |---|---|
  | ~30 | Thesis |
  | ~100 | Story 1: a named person → what you did with their help → paying it forward |
  | ~100 | Story 2: the same arc |
  | ~70 | UK plan: specific communities and organisations |
- **Must include:** names, who made the first contact, value flowing both ways, measurable outcomes, UK specifics.

### studying_in_uk: studying in the UK (300)
- **Tests:** "researched your three chosen courses and, **focusing your answer only on your first choice course**, explain how it connects to your background, career aspirations, and the impact you hope to make."
- ⚠️ **Common outdated advice.** Older guides told applicants to give reasons for all three courses. Current guidance says focus on the **first choice only**, while still showing that all three were researched.
- **Beat plan:**

  | Budget | Beat |
  |---|---|
  | ~60 | What you've done in the field |
  | ~35 | The gap ("despite this, I lack…") |
  | ~150 | First-choice course: named modules, approach, faculty → your background, career and impact |
  | ~20 | One line showing the other two were researched (optional) |
  | ~35 | Network and added value |

### career_plan: career plan (300)
- **Tests:** "a **short, mid, and long-term** career plan which has clear, measurable goals" and "a genuine willingness to **collaborate with the UK** in tackling shared global and local challenges."
- **Beat plan:**

  | Budget | Beat |
  |---|---|
  | ~30 | How the degree takes you to the next level |
  | ~80 | Short term (0-2 years after return): concrete SMART actions |
  | ~80 | Mid term (2-5 years) |
  | ~80 | Long term: the life dream, with one vivid scenario and numbers |
  | ~30 | UK collaboration + a beyond-self close |
- ⚠️ Older guides use a short- and long-term structure only. Current guidance asks for **short, mid and long**, plus **UK collaboration**.

## AI policy
> "Using artificial intelligence software to generate answers to Chevening's essay questions is strictly prohibited."

- Chevening uses detection tools for "fraud, plagiarism, and AI-generated content".
- Applicants **attest at submission** that they didn't use AI to create their responses.
- Breaches mean disqualification.

The skill must state this once when drafting for Chevening. The user decides how to use any draft.

## Interview / selection
- A competency-based panel interview in English, run by British embassy or high commission representatives.
- It covers the application, aspirations and impact, the three course choices, and university application status.
- Location and format are confirmed at booking.
- Bring official photo ID.
- Use the mock interview mode with the "Chevening embassy panel" persona.

## Common mistakes specific to Chevening
- Using a story twice across the four essays. The panel reads them together, so run `check_consistency.py`.
- Contradicting dates between essays (e.g. a fellowship year).
- Listing titles in the leadership essay instead of showing influence.
- Giving equal space to all three courses. Focus on the first choice.
- A career plan with no UK collaboration angle, or no mid-term goals.
- Not meeting the 2,800-hour work requirement, or not meeting the return commitment.
