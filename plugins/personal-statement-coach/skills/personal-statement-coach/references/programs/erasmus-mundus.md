# Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters

> **Verify live:** there is **no central application**. Each consortium (joint master's) runs its own application, documents, limits and deadline, usually between October and January for the following year. The 1,000-word budget is a common limit, not a rule.

## Official sources
- Catalogue of programs: https://www.eacea.ec.europa.eu/scholarships/erasmus-mundus-catalogue_en
- Then the specific program's own website (the authoritative source).

## Who it's for
- Integrated master's degrees taught across two or more European universities, usually leading to a joint or double degree.
- Many offer EU-funded scholarships. Some don't, because their funding has ended, so check.

## Essays and what each one tests
### motivation_letter
Most consortia ask some version of:
- why this joint master's,
- why the mobility track (which universities, and in what order),
- your academic and professional preparation,
- your career plan.

**Tests:** fit with the program's *specific* specialisation tracks, and readiness for multi-country, multicultural study.

**Beat plan (≈ 1,000 words):**

| Budget | Beat |
|---|---|
| ~150 | Hook: a story from your work |
| ~200 | Preparation |
| ~300 | **Why this program + your mobility track**: named specialisations and universities, each tied to a need from your story |
| ~150 | Added value to a multinational cohort |
| ~200 | SMART return plan, and impact beyond yourself |

**Program-specific trap:** when applying to several Erasmus Mundus programs, each letter's "why this program" must be genuinely specific. Use Adapt mode, not copy-paste.

## AI policy
Set by each consortium; there is no central policy on the catalogue page. Check the program's FAQ and declarations.
