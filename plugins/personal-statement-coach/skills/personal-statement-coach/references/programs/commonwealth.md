# Commonwealth Master's Scholarships (Commonwealth Scholarship Commission in the UK)

> **Verify live:** the word budgets in the front matter are working defaults. The official page lists the sections without limits; the application system sets them. **No direct applications**: you apply through a **nominator** (your National Nominating Agency or an approved NGO or charity), which has its own deadline.

## Official sources
- Commonwealth Master's Scholarships: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/commonwealth-masters-scholarships/

## Who it's for
- A UK master's for citizens of eligible low- and middle-income Commonwealth countries, focused on **development impact** at home.

## Selection criteria (official)
1. "Candidate merit (including academic experience)"
2. "Quality of research proposal" / study plan
3. "Potential impact on the development of the candidate's home country"

## Essays and what each one tests
### development_impact (four parts, official)
1. How the scholarship relates to "development issues at the global, national, and local level" and to the chosen **CSC development theme**.
2. How you'll apply your new skills after the scholarship ends.
3. The expected development changes: "the outcomes that they aim to achieve", the timeframe, and the beneficiaries.
4. How the impact "could be best measured and evidenced".

This is pure SMART and beyond-self. Use numbers, timeframes and named beneficiaries. Link to the SDGs honestly.

### study_plan
"A detailed plan of study": the course, modules, and how they build the skills for your impact plan.

### career_plans
- Award objectives and how each will be met.
- Career plans for **the five years after the scholarship**.
- Long-term plans.

### personal_statement
"How your personal background has encouraged you to want to make an impact in your home country", including contributions made and **barriers overcome**. This is V-SPICE and "challenges in proportion".
