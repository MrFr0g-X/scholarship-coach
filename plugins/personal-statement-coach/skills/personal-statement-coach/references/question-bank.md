## 1. Coaching interview questions

Ask 3-5 per round. After every answer, push: **"When exactly? How many? Who? What changed because of you?"**

### Purpose and "why" (Rule P1 and Part 1 item 1)
- If you got this scholarship and it changed nothing for anyone but you, would you still want it? Why?
- What problem in your community or country makes you angry enough to work on it?
- Finish this sentence honestly: "The world will be better when…"
- Which SDG is closest to the work you already do?

### Identity and self-awareness
- How would three people who've worked with you describe you, and what example would each give?
- What is something you do that most people in your field don't?
- What did you learn about yourself from a person you couldn't work with?
- What are you proudest of that isn't on your CV?

### Proof: achievements and signaling
- What is the most selective thing you've ever been chosen for? How many applied?
- Have you ever been recognised by an official, organisation or media outlet? When?
- What numbers describe your impact: people trained, money raised, followers or reach, sales, users, hours?
- Is there a "first" or "only" anywhere in your story? (e.g. "only Egyptian among 30")

### Stories (for STAR)
- Tell me about a time you were responsible for something that nearly failed. What exactly did you do?
- When did you lead people who didn't have to follow you?
- When did you start something nobody asked you to start?
- Take your most ordinary volunteer, work or school role. Who benefited downstream from it? (This is the reframe technique.)

### Challenges (V, P)
- What did you have to overcome that your peers didn't (money, region, school type, family, health, being first-generation)?
- What's the achievement that means more *because* of where you started?
- When did you want to quit, and what made you keep going?

### People and networking
- Who opened a door for you? How did you meet them, and what did they do?
- Whose door have you opened since? What happened to them?
- Who in the destination country would you most like to meet or work with, and why?

### Fit and research
- Name two modules, professors or labs in the program and the specific reason each matters to your plan.
- What does this program give you that one in your own country can't?
- What gap in your knowledge or skills does this degree fill? ("Despite all I did, I lack…")

### Future (SMART, 5 years)
- What will you do in your first 3 months back home?
- Where will you, and your organisation or project, be in 5 years? What number will prove it?
- What is the "life dream" version? Describe one concrete scenario of it working (who, where, how many, how much).

### Added value
- The cohort will be 40 people from 30 countries. What problem will they have that you can solve? (Facilitation, translation, medicine, photography, event-running, tech…)

## 2. Playbooks by question type

### Program-specific essays (Chevening, Gates, Commonwealth, DAAD, …)
Program-specific beat plans live in `programs/<id>.md` (see `programs/INDEX.md`). Read the profile first; the generic types below cover everything else.

### University PS / Statement of Purpose (650-1,000 words unless a limit is set)
| Paragraph | Job |
|---|---|
| ¶1 | **Hook:** the moment or problem that pulled you into the field |
| ¶2 | **Academic preparation:** degree, key courses, research, grades only if strong |
| ¶3 | **Practical experience:** work, projects, leadership (as STAR) |
| ¶4 | **Why this program:** named modules, faculty and resources, matched to your goals |
| ¶5 | **Goals and contribution:** short and long term, beyond yourself |

### Undergrad application
| Paragraph | Job |
|---|---|
| ¶1 | Why this subject, through a specific experience |
| ¶2 | School achievements, and what you learned from each |
| ¶3 | Extracurriculars, and *your development after each one* (Feedback #14) |
| ¶4 | Why this university or country |
| ¶5 | What you'll do with it |

### Exchange program / short answers (100-300 words)
- Answer exactly what's asked in the first sentence or two.
- One story, then one sentence on what you'll bring to the group, then one sentence on how you'll use it back home.
- Model: "How did your project change you personally?" → a short list of results → "You might now wonder why… The answer is [the project]." See `examples.md` §3.

### Job or internship motivation letter
| Paragraph | Job |
|---|---|
| ¶1 | Why *this* organisation, with a researched specific (project, value, recent news) |
| ¶2 | 2-3 proofs matched to the role's top needs (STAR-lite, with numbers) |
| ¶3 | What you'll contribute in the first months |
| ¶4 | A short close; for a letter format, a sign-off is fine here |

## 3. Classic questions (interviews and short answers)
| Question | Approach |
|---|---|
| Who are you? | Self-awareness: values, strengths, one proof, your purpose. 30-second elevator-pitch structure. |
| Why this program? | Match your reason to the program's stated goal (leadership program → leadership growth). |
| Why this country? | Specific: institutions, sector strengths, the people you want to learn from. |
| Why should we select you? | Tailor to the scholarship's goals. Use your qualities + proudest accomplishment + edge over others. Not humble. |
| What will you contribute to the group? | Added value: the group's likely problem → your skill that solves it. |
| How will this affect you? | How it benefits you **and** lets you benefit others. "It's not a bonbon." |
| How will you give back? | Beyond yourself: community, country, SDGs, with concrete plans. |
| Where will you be in 5 years? | SMART, ambitious, specific. |
| A person you admire? | Pick 2-3 qualities and connect them to your own path. |
| What are your activities? | Book club, volunteering, reading on X. Never cafés, the beach or your fiancé. |
